A one-line entry for make.fun's "The Best Meal You Ever Had" bounty. `main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).

## Why this line

The picture is a single kitchen-table scene on the night before leaving home for university: a plate of forty pork-and-chive dumplings — a number big enough to say "eat, there is plenty, don't hold back" without anyone having to say it out loud. The sensory details do the emotional work in the middle of the sentence: the sharp bite of black vinegar on the tongue, and the steam rising off the freshly opened plate straight into your eyes.

The closing turn is the double meaning of that steam. The narrator's eyes sting from the vapor, and the mother — who is not ready to cry in front of her child on the last night — pretends the steam stung hers too. Both of them know; neither of them says. The best meal here is not really about the dumplings: it is about a goodbye that a family chose to serve as dinner instead.
